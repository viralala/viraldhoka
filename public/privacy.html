Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Viral Dhoka. It has no accounts, no forms and no ads. This page says exactly what it keeps.

What is stored in your browser

  • Your language choice (vd-lang in local storage), so the site opens in the language you picked. It never leaves your device.
  • Your cookie choice (vd-consent in local storage), so you are not asked again.

Analytics

If analytics is switched on, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which projects people look at. It only loads after you click Accept on the cookie banner. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_* for up to 2 years, with IP addresses anonymised by Google. If you click Decline, nothing is loaded. You can change your mind any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web server, Vercel briefly processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and stop abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Project links open other websites with their own policies. This site sends them no data beyond what your browser normally sends (and no full page address, thanks to a strict referrer policy).

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the GDPR you can ask what is held about you and have it deleted. Since this site holds nothing on its servers, clearing your browser's site data removes everything. For questions, email viralnotviral@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
